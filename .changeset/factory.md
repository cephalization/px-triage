Integrate Phoenix's factory. Bug and feature suggestions say whether the
factory would accept the issue (mirroring `dispatch.py` and the
factory-candidate policy) and recommend it when Jev reads the issue as small
and checkable in a sandbox. `F` in the triage session, the factory option in
the `b`/`f` flows, and `pxt apply --factory` add the `factory` label and assign
nobody. Issues without a size label get `size:S`/`size:M`/`size:L` from a new
Jev effort question. `pxt team` gains three buckets: factory PRs ready for
review (with their review depth), factory runs waiting on a maintainer, and
`factory-candidate` issues to start with `F`.
